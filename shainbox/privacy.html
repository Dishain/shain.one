Privacy & data

Privacy.

The short version: ShainBox is local-first. Your library lives as plain folders on your disk, the app needs no account, and this site collects only anonymous analytics. No selling, no ads, no surprises.

LAST UPDATED · 2026-07-05
§ 01

What we collect

Almost nothing — and none of your actual content. Here's the full picture:

  • THE APP ShainBox runs entirely on your device. Your boards are plain folders on your disk, and the app needs no account. We never see what you store in it.
  • THIS SITE Anonymized usage analytics — page views, clicks, scroll depth, viewport size and country-level location. No personal data, and we don't record anything you type.
  • DOWNLOADS & PURCHASE Downloads are served by GitHub. When you buy a licence, a payment provider processes the transaction and passes us your email and an order reference. We never receive your full card number.
§ 02

Why we collect it

To run and improve this site. Anonymous analytics show which sections work, where people drop off, and which devices to design for.

To deliver the app and your licence. Your email and order reference let us send your licence and receipt, and help if you write in for support.

Nothing else. We don't sell data, run ads, or build profiles. That's a promise, not a setting.

§ 03

Third-party services

A few services help us ship this. Each sees only what it needs:

  • CLARITY Microsoft Clarity records anonymized session behaviour on this site. No PII. Their privacy statement.
  • GITHUB Hosts this site and serves app downloads (GitHub Pages & Releases). Logs basic request data such as IP and user agent. Their privacy statement.
  • PAYMENTS Our payment provider processes purchases and keeps billing details on their side. We only receive your email and an order reference.
§ 04

Your rights

Access or delete your data. Email web.shain@gmail.com with what you'd like — we'll send whatever we have on file, correct it, or wipe it within 7 days.

If you're in the EU/EEA or UK, GDPR applies and these rights are legally yours. We treat everyone the same way regardless of location.

§ 05

Contact

Questions, requests, or a bug in this policy — write to web.shain@gmail.com. A real human reads every email.

ShainBox is a personal project by Dmytro Lehkov. There's no company entity behind it yet.
